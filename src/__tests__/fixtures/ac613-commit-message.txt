CC-826: Decide claim glob overlap exactly and name the colliding pair (#613)

## Summary

Slice 1 of CC-826 (plan: `claude-channels/sources/CC-826-plan.md`). Also covers CC-695, CC-685, CC-666 and CC-294.

## What changes
- `patternsOverlap` (src/broker/claims.ts) decides glob-vs-glob overlap exactly, segment by segment, with a memoised walk. Interior `**` takes zero or more segments and trailing `**` one or more. Two `*` segments intersect when their fixed heads and tails are prefix- and suffix-compatible. If either side is an exact path, the answer is `matchPattern`'s and the code never falls through to a prefix test. `x/` is read as `x/**`. A `**` inside a longer segment is still reported as an overlap (conservative).
- The old heuristics are removed: `isShallow`, `shallowOverlap`, `directoryOf` and `literalPrefix`.
- A refusal names the specific pair: `"alice" holds src/cli/** (overlaps your src/cli/args.ts)`.
- The cap message in `src/args.ts` suggests `dir/**` or `dir/name*`. Both goldens are updated to match.

## Changes

- **CC-826: Decide claim glob overlap exactly and name the colliding pair.** patternsOverlap fell back to directory and literal-prefix heuristics for wildcard pairs, so disjoint globs in one directory (config*.ts beside gate-resolve-round*) were always refused. It now walks both patterns segment by segment: interior ** takes zero or more segments, trailing ** one or more, and two * segments meet when their fixed heads and tails agree. An exact path is asked of the other glob and never falls through.

  A refusal now names the held pattern and the wanted one that overlap, and the pattern-cap message suggests a directory or prefix glob.

  Covers CC-695, CC-685, CC-666 and CC-294.
